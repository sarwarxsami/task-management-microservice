anti-social-media by design

A quiet place for your tasks.

  • Nothing calls you back. No notifications, no emails, no reminders. Nothing from here reaches you when you are away.
  • Nobody talks here. No messages, no comments, no likes, no followers, no "seen".
  • You write, and you let go. You keep your tasks and choose who may see them. Whether anyone ever looks, you will never know.
  • So there is nothing to check. No one returns to see who reacted. Everyone is on their own, together with everyone else.
  • Every visit is a choice. Nothing pulls anyone in, so whoever is here came by themselves. If someone reads your task, they went looking for it, and that is what makes it count.

How it works

1 · Your tasks

Add a task with a title, and if you like a description, a deadline and a priority (low, medium, high). Tick the box when it is done; untick to reopen it.

Edit and Delete sit on every task. Clear completed removes all your finished tasks at once.

The boxes on top count your tasks: total, active, completed, due today and overdue. Search, sort (newest, deadline, priority, title) and the All / Active / Completed / Overdue buttons help you find things. Overdue tasks are marked in red.

2 · Who can see a task

Every task has a visibility:

  • Only Me (the default): nobody else, ever.
  • Trusted: people on Your Task Viewers list (see 3).
  • Open: every logged-in user, in Discover and in your Space.

A Trusted task can go further:

  • All Trusted: everyone on Your Task Viewers.
  • Hand-picked: only the people you tick from that list.
  • Group: everyone in the groups you tick, Default included.

The Who can see button on a Trusted task lists exactly who that is right now. Nobody is told when you share something with them.

3 · Your Task Viewers and groups

On the Viewers page you choose the people who may see your Trusted tasks. Add a Listed user by username. It is one-way: adding someone does not let you see their tasks.

Sort them into groups with Create group, then Add member, Rename or Delete. A person can be in several groups; whoever is in none is in Default.

If you remove someone, they lose access at once, and deleting a group never shows a task to more people than before.

It works the other way too: anyone who added you appears under View Tasks, and you can Leave their list there at any time. They are not told.

4 · Discover, Spaces and Listed / Unlisted

Every account starts Unlisted: hidden from Discover, with a Space only you can open. Switch to Listed in Account to appear in Discover and let others open your Space.

Discover shows Listed people and a feed of Open tasks. Click a name to open their Space: their username, name and the tasks you are allowed to see. Everything there is read-only.

Open tasks of Unlisted people still appear, marked "User is Unlisted".

5 · Viewer Access Requests

Want to see someone's Trusted tasks? Send a Viewer Access Request from Discover or their Space. It carries no message. They can accept or decline, and you can cancel it.

Requests wait quietly on the Viewers page; the small number on the Viewers button is the only sign of them. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Once accepted, their Trusted tasks shared with you show up under View Tasks, where you can also leave their list again.

6 · Your account and privacy

You log in with your student ID, which is never shown to anyone, admins included. Others only see your name and username.

In Account you can change your name, username and password, switch Listed / Unlisted, replay the tour, or delete your account with all of your tasks.

Admins can only see each account's name and username and delete accounts. They cannot read your tasks.
